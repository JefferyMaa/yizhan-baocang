装车小站长 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

1. 我们收集什么

装车小站长是单机游戏，不需要注册账号，不收集你的姓名、手机号、位置、通讯录等个人信息。

游戏进度（已完成的关卡、星级、音效设置、每关开过的局数）只保存在你自己的设备上：网页版用浏览器的本地存储，小游戏版用平台提供的本地存储。我们收不到这些数据。

2. 广告

在接入广告的平台上（例如微信、抖音、Telegram 和海外网页游戏平台），广告由平台或它的广告服务商提供。它们可能按照各自的隐私政策收集设备信息，用来展示和统计广告。我们不会把你的个人信息提供给广告方。激励视频只在你主动点击时播放。

3. 第三方平台

通过微信、抖音、Telegram 等平台打开游戏时，平台按它自己的规则处理你的账号信息。本游戏不读取你的平台头像、昵称等账号信息。

4. 未成年人

本游戏适合 8 岁及以上的用户。未成年人请在监护人的指导下使用。

5. 删除数据

清除浏览器的网站数据，或在平台里删除这个小游戏，就会删除本地保存的进度。

6. 联系我们

对本政策有疑问，请在 GitHub 提交问题。

7. 政策更新

本政策更新时会在这个页面公布，并修改上方的更新日期。

Parcel Station Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. What we collect

Parcel Station is a single-player game. It needs no account and does not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts or other personal information.

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, sound setting and how many times you have played each level) is stored only on your own device, in your browser's local storage or the local storage provided by the platform. We never receive it.

2. Ads

On platforms that show ads (such as WeChat, Douyin, Telegram and web game portals), ads are served by the platform or its ad partners. They may collect device information under their own privacy policies to show and measure ads. We do not share your personal information with advertisers. Rewarded videos play only when you tap to watch them.

3. Third-party platforms

When you open the game through WeChat, Douyin, Telegram or another platform, that platform handles your account under its own rules. The game does not read your profile picture, nickname or other account information.

4. Children

The game is suitable for players aged 8 and up. Younger players should play under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

5. Deleting your data

Clearing the site data in your browser, or removing the game from the platform, deletes the progress saved on your device.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub.

7. Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date above.
